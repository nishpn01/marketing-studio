# Session story mining — manual source-led skill

**Use when:** the owner shares a permitted Claude Code, Codex or ChatGPT conversation transcript, or asks for editorial ideas from a working session.

**Required:** explicit user-provided/authorized transcript access, intended business workspace, sensitivity guidance and source pointers. Do not claim cross-app access to unshared chats.

1. Read the actual conversation. Extract the owner's specific question, uncertainty, alternatives considered, corrections and conclusions. Distinguish the owner's words from assistant suggestions.
2. Corroborate observable events with commits, documentation or independent sources when available. Mark unverifiable experiences `[NEED OWNER CONFIRMATION]`.
3. Assign **privacy** (private / usable with paraphrase / owner-approved public); default to private.
4. Create a story card in the business workspace with: source and permissions, factual spine, real tension, counterargument, takeaway, plausible reader and creative angle options.
5. Avoid writing a newsletter if the session yielded no worthwhile insight. Do not turn private customer conversations, secrets or personal history into public content without explicit clearance.
6. Let the Newsletter/SEO/Video specialist choose the most useful format and request the owner's angle approval.

**Output:** one private source-linked story card plus 2–3 distinct angles, with unknowns explicitly listed. Never place private material in the reusable core.
