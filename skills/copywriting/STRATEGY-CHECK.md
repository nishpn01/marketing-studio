# Copywriting: research and strategy check

**Purpose:** Operational, task-selective thinking for the Studio Director. This is not a new agent, a permission grant, a fixed template, or a public business fact. Provenance for the selected methods stays in `the omitted source study` and `RESEARCH-WORKFLOW.md`.

Before recommending an angle, ask:
1. **Business truth:** What is available, proposed, observed, or untested? Don't promote roadmap claims or AI-generated personas into user evidence.
2. **Reader and awareness:** Who is this for, what problem or curiosity do they recognize, and what knowledge do they already have? Mark unsupported audience assumptions explicitly.
3. **Authentic voice of customer:** Which permitted original questions, reviews, support interactions, comments or interviews reveal pains, objections or language? If there are none, say so; never invent quotes.
4. **Alternatives:** What does existing credible coverage or an alternative offer explain? Verify actual differences instead of claiming generic competitive superiority.
5. **Value and proof:** What relevant insight, mechanism, benefit or decision can this asset *support*? Pair each material claim with original evidence and privacy clearance.
6. **Message and positioning:** Choose one central reader question and a credible promise; compare at least two meaningfully different angles. The strongest option isn't always worth producing.
7. **Appropriate action:** Commercial copy may need a real offer/CTA. An educational issue may offer a learning exercise or a question; don't force scarcity or sales pressure.

**Existing newsletter Director schema:** Express this judgment through `reason`, `reader_question`, `angle`, `alternatives_considered`, `reader_promise`, `evidence_plan`, `exclusions`, and `open_questions_for_owner`. Do not invent output keys or change the Runner contract. If essential evidence is missing, return `insufficient_evidence`.

Research is selective and proportional: a personal evidence-backed issue does not need fictitious demographics or a forced competitor audit. The Director owns strategy; the owner retains approval, and business context is guidance rather than citable evidence. No publishing, spending, credentials or shared-policy changes are authorized by this skill.
