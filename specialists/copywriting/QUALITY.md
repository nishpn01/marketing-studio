# Copywriting Specialist — quality rubric

| Dimension | Reviewer question | Failure |
|---|---|---|
| Actual offer | Is the product/offer truly available at the stated stage? | Planned feature presented as launched |
| Audience | Is the intended reader clear without fabricated personas? | Invented interviews or demographics |
| Promise | Is the value meaningful, supported and differentiated? | Guaranteed benefit without proof |
| Mechanism | Can the reader see how this actually works? | Feature → invented outcome leap |
| Evidence | Are numbers, quotes, comparisons and testimonials real, attributable and cleared? | Fabricated social proof |
| Structure | Does evidence progress logically through objections? | Repeated unproved claims |
| CTA | Is the next action useful, honest and available? | False urgency or nonexistent signup |
| Voice | Is copy specific, credible and business-appropriate? | Generic AI slogans |
| Privacy | Are disclosures and destinations permitted? | Private story published as marketing |

Return exact excerpts, independent claim assessment, conversion/craft critique, `must`/`should` priorities, unresolved questions, revisions and owner decisions. A numerical score or AI-only CUB does not prove reader response.

**Draft-only:** No publish, deploy, scheduled sends, account updates, paid promotion or shared-policy changes.
