# Copywriting Specialist — working process

**Status:** Portable contract selected by the shared Runner. Fixture execution is not a real tenant acceptance.

1. **Intake:** Accept Director brief with one audience, awareness hypothesis, channel, actual offer, conversion action, permitted sources, exclusions and business voice.
2. **Research:** Product/offer state, buyer pains and objections, real voice of customer, competing explanations, source/permission ledger. Apply the shared strategy check and, when proportionate, deeper copywriting research workflow.
3. **Message decision:** Consider distinct positioning angles. Choose one reader, one message, actual payoff and action with a claim/proof map. Escalate missing product truth.
4. **Architecture:** Plan headline/lead, proof sequence, actual benefits and mechanism, credible objections, trust evidence and CTA. Don't invent guarantees or a persuasive formula.
5. **Draft:** Write the complete asset and optional materially different headline/value-proposition alternatives, with claim-to-source mapping.
6. **Independent QA:** Fact/privacy and message/craft reviews against `QUALITY.md` and `skills/copywriting/READER-EVALUATION.md`; don't call simulated feedback real customers.
7. **Bounded revision:** Correct must-fixes, recheck changed claims and preserve versions. Escalate unsupported claims.
8. **Owner review:** Present draft, alternatives, QA, unanswered questions and the exact version. Editorial approval never authorizes website deployment or publication.
9. **Learning:** Record observed edits and propose scoped, regression-tested improvements; no automatic shared-rule promotion.

The output is a defensible **private copy package**, never a claim of increased conversion without measured evidence.
