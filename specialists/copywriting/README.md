# Copywriting specialization

Shared, reusable **Copywriting** methods are separated into three operational capabilities:
- [Research and strategy](../../skills/copywriting/STRATEGY-CHECK.md) — directly pinned to the live newsletter Director.
- [Message craft](../../skills/copywriting/MESSAGE-CRAFT.md) — directly pinned to the live newsletter writer.
- [Reader evaluation](../../skills/copywriting/READER-EVALUATION.md) — directly pinned to the live editorial reviewer.

Source attributions and the optional detailed research workflow remain in `skills/copywriting/`, not injected wholesale into every model prompt. This is an **independently written, selective adaptation informed by several sources**: newsletter expertise is not replaced by sales techniques and the fact reviewer does not get persuasion tactics.

[AGENT.md](AGENT.md), [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md), and [QUALITY.md](QUALITY.md) define the **Conversion Copywriting Specialist** for website and sales copy. The shared Runner selects it through its pack manifest (`packs/conversion-copywriting`); synthetic fixtures exercise execution, factual QA, craft QA and owner review. Real tenant acceptance remains separate.

No real business evidence, paid course text or tenant copy lives in the reusable Core.
