# Newsletter editorial quality rubric v1

Use a mix of evidence-based checks and human evaluation; a total numerical score alone does not establish quality.

| Dimension | Reviewer question | Failure example |
|---|---|---|
| Truth | Can each meaningful factual claim be traced to original evidence? | Invented founder scene or product usage statistic |
| Original insight | What could this publication say that generic AI content cannot? | Familiar platitudes without concrete experience or artifact |
| Audience specificity | Why does this particular reader care now? | Builder issue written like general job-search advice |
| Story & argument | Is there authentic tension, development and a point? | Invented conflict or mechanical chronological changelog |
| Craft | Does each section do work? Are rhythm, transitions and ending earned? | Repeated section lengths, tidy slogans at every ending |
| /unslop editorial pass | Which actual excerpts use generic language, forced contrast, symmetrical prose, filler or empty metaphors? Were they improved without factual or voice damage? | Mechanical ban-list green but source-thin, synthetic prose still survives |
| Utility | What does the reader understand, try, question or feel differently? | Vague motivational conclusion |
| Voice | Does it sound believable for the actual author, without guru language or borrowed professional authority? | Generic "AI thought leader" prose; pretending to be a recruiter or seasoned expert |
| Calibration | Are findings appropriately scoped and any promises actually supported? | Guaranteed results, universal advice or treating a prototype test as validated business success |
| Privacy and promotion | Is every disclosure approved? Is marketing claim stage correct? | Personal data or claiming an unlaunched feature is live |

## Required report
- Named reviewer and /unslop method or skill version/reference; exact flagged snippets with context and edits. No claim of automatic skill invocation when it was a manual review.
- Flag the difference between literal banned-word searches and substantive editorial diagnosis. A zero-match linter result is **not** a quality pass.
- BLOCK / REVISE / READY_FOR_OWNER_REVIEW (verdict names are defined in the quality gate contract) with specific citations or source links.
- What genuinely improved from the old version.
- What got worse or remains uncertain.
- Which feedback is an *asset-level preference* versus a proposed reusable skill rule.
- Owner evaluation and follow-up regression after skill changes.

A draft with unsupported testimony fails regardless of literary polish.
