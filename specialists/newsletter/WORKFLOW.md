# Newsletter specialist workflow (manual pilot)

Input: approved business workspace, audience, editorial program, desired reader outcome, source pointers, privacy boundaries. Baseline draft optional.

**Step 0: Research before sources become a draft.** Read `skills/copywriting/RESEARCH-WORKFLOW.md` and complete a private dated research pack: business/product truth; actual reader question and voice-of-customer with links (or explicitly missing); current brand and author position; comparable editorial coverage; source/permission map; research synthesis. A generated journal may be an *unapproved* summary: corroborate and request owner voice confirmation. Do not treat it as sufficient standalone story research.

**Step 1: Discovery.** Extract the actual problem, decision tension, failed hypothesis, credible result, reader problem and missing details. If asked "what should we post?" recommend ranked angles with evidence and publishability; no quota-driven draft.

**Step 2: Angle selection.** Choose one reader, one core idea, optional proportionate reader action. Name rejected angles and why they would be weaker. If no evidence-backed angle is worthwhile, recommend no issue.

**Step 3: Brief.** Record 3–5 actual source facts, their claim IDs, potential interpretations and allowed public level. Choose a flexible proof order; do not use a fixed headline/lesson/action template.

**Step 4: Draft.** Write an original newsletter issue in the workspace's defined voice. The author is not a guru by default: no manufactured credentials, guarantees, universal 'best practices' or promises that career/marketing outcomes will follow. Don't manufacture emotional state or scenes. Keep business product stage honest. Avoid formulaic equal-section rhythm.

**Step 5: Independent factual review.** Reviewer reads original sources and checks evidence, timeline, claims, audience context and privacy. Do not treat a prose linter as source verification.

**Step 5b: Independent /unslop review.** Apply `skills/prose-unslop-review/SKILL.md` and the original /unslop skill if installed and permitted. Scan stock vocabulary, rhetorical contrasts, forced triads, generic end-of-section wisdom, repeated paragraph shapes, meaningless transition lines and filler. Diagnose the source of each problem, revise locally, compare before and after. **Do not manufacture new facts or flatten distinctive author voice to satisfy regex.** Re-check factual claims after edits.

**Step 5c: Reader/owner CUB.** Test which passages are confusing, unbelievable or boring. An AI self-review is preliminary, not proof of quality. Use the Swappy check as an additional editorial question.

**Step 6: Mechanical validation.** Run the zero-dependency metadata gate. It catches absent source IDs, missing claim map, explicit placeholders and invalid pilot status; it never substitutes for source checking or owner approval.

**Step 7: Feedback and comparison.** Compare to baseline with the same source data. Record wins, losses and changes requiring owner confirmation. Create a *proposed* scoped improvement and regression cases; do not auto-rewrite skills.

**Outputs:** research pack, angle memo, approved or proposed brief, draft, manifest, QA/baseline review, owner questions. Store only in private business workspace; no public sends or account writes.

**Model runtime:** Claude Code/Codex/ChatGPT can follow this Markdown manually. This manual workflow does not require the `studio` CLI or Service, which now exist.
